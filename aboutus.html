Angkor DC

Angkor DC is the digital cinema of Cambodia – a streaming service that provides opportunity to Cambodian people around the world to watch Cambodian films at your own place no matter where you are, any time you like.

Our Goal

Angkor DC gives Cambodian films a wider audience, in Cambodia and abroad - building a stronger local film industry and creating more opportunities for Cambodian film producers, actors, actresses and all film crews. This wider audience will help raise film quality.
